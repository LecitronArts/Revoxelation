<objective>
Fix HIGH bug: egui scratch buffers have GPU-use-after-free with double-buffered
frames. The single Vec cleanup can free buffers while the GPU is still reading them.
Switch to a per-frame ring (2 Vecs) so buffers are only freed after the correct
frame fence has been waited on.
</objective>

<locked_decisions>
- D-01: Replace `scratch_buffers: Vec<(vk::Buffer, Allocation)>` with `scratch_buffers: [Vec<(vk::Buffer, Allocation)>; 2]` (2 = FRAMES_IN_FLIGHT).
- D-02: `free_stale_scratch(current_frame)` frees `scratch_buffers[current_frame]` — which was populated 2 frames ago (since we just waited on this frame's fence). This is safe because the fence wait at the top of submit_frame guarantees the GPU is done with this frame slot's previous work.
- D-03: `paint()` accepts `current_frame: usize` parameter. Records new buffers into `scratch_buffers[current_frame]`.
- D-04: On destroy(), free ALL scratch buffer slots (both frames).
</locked_decisions>
</context>
