<objective>
Fix CRITICAL bug: Hi-Z pyramid is not recreated on swapchain resize,
causing GPU reads from destroyed depth image (validation errors / GPU crash).
After this fix, resizing the window correctly rebuilds the Hi-Z pyramid
with new dimensions and re-registers its resources in the bindless table.
</objective>

<locked_decisions>
- D-01: HiZPyramid::recreate(self, renderer, w, h) already exists — it destroys old resources and calls ::new(). The method takes self by value.
- D-02: recreate_swapchain_context takes &mut Renderer. After depth image recreation, take hiz_pyramid Option, call recreate(), put it back.
- D-03: After Hi-Z recreation, re-register full_view + sampler at BindlessTable binding 7 via register_image.
- D-04: HiZPyramid::destroy calls allocator.free — must happen before allocator is borrowed for new creation. Sequence: take hiz → destroy → create new hiz → store.
</locked_decisions>
